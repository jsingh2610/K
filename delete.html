Kailasa — Account & Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and make it easy to delete your account and all associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, send an email to:

kailasaapp@gmail.com

Include the email address you used to sign in. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your email address and authentication credentials
  • All cloud-synced progress (streaks, journal entries, badges)
  • Any stored preferences and app settings
  • Purchase association records

After deletion, this action cannot be undone.

Data Retained After Deletion

  • Google Play purchase transaction records (managed by Google)
  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics that cannot identify you

Contact

Questions about data deletion? Email kailasaapp@gmail.com
