# Ghar Kailash: the family series (50 scripts, F01 to F50)

The 36 mythic stories say why. This series says: and here is where it meets your Tuesday.
One ordinary Indian family. Real stress. Shiva never fixes the problem with magic. He shows up, quietly, and the person does one small thing differently.

| Who | Age | Life | What they carry | Look (rig notes) |

**Only Pari and Dadi can see Nandi.** Everyone can feel Shiva, each in their own way; only at the very end of the series do they all see him.

## How Shiva appears (pick ONE per script; never more)
1. **The roof**: seated on the water tank under the moon, back to us or in profile.
2. **The shelf**: the small black lingam on the home shrine shelf glows; his face shows faintly on the wall behind.
3. **The stranger**: an auto driver, a chai seller, a man on the next bench. Ordinary clothes, but blue-grey skin in shadow, a rudraksha on the wrist, a crescent-shaped scar or pendant. He says one line and is gone.
4. **The reflection**: in a phone's black screen, a tea cup, a window at night.
5. **The plate**: a two-second flash of the matching mythic story (re-use that reel's art) dropped into the scene like a memory.

He speaks at most ONE line (voice `s`). Often he says nothing.

## Shape of every family script (24 to 34 s after speed-up; 55 to 80 words of VO)
1. **Hook (0 to 3.5 s)**: the stress, at its sharpest, as a picture; HOOK TEXT in the viewer's own words ("you"). Hook Law applies in full.
2. **The squeeze (to 40 percent)**: two or three beats that make it worse. Specific and Indian: the EMI message, the relative on the phone, the class group chat.
3. **TURN**: Shiva's one appearance.
4. **The small act**: one thing the person does. A breath. Phone face-down. Tea drunk hot. A hand on a shoulder. Eleven names on the mala. Never a miracle: the loan is still there.
5. **Close**: teaching in under ten words + "That is sadhana." then the CTA.

## Rules
- The problem is NOT solved. The person is steadier. Say so in the picture (the pile of bills is still on the table).
- No medical, exam or money promises. Never "pray and it will be fixed".
- For the heavier ones (F25, F28, F40, F42, F46) add to the caption: "If it is heavy, talk to someone you trust."
- The app appears in about one script in three, in the person's hand, doing what it really does: the japa counter, the 40-day path, the sankalpa, Whisper to Nandi. Marked `APP:`.
- Nandi cameo and the hidden face stay (the face is on household things: a fridge magnet, a calendar, a steel dabba lid).
- Narrator is the same calm voice. Family members do not speak in their own voices; the narrator quotes them.
